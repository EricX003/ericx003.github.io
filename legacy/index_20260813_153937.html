Eric Xing

I'm a 4th year PhD student in the Multimodal Vision Research Lab at WashU advised by Nathan Jacobs. I've generally been working on multimodal learning and search. I did my BS in Computer Science with a minor in Math at Western Kentucky University.

Email  /  CV  /  LinkedIn  /  Scholar  /  GitHub

Research

Lately, my research has spanned vision-language models and multimodal learning. I am currently interested in efficient search paradigms, multimodal reranking, and hierarchical representation learning.
